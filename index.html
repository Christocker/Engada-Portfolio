About.txt

Electronics engineering student at De La Salle University – Dasmariñas, founder of OneByte, and a developer who builds across hardware, web, and data.

I run OneByte and OneByte Steel Cabinets — a digital products business and a steel-cabinet dealership where I handle suppliers, inventory, logistics, customer relations, and the company website. In the lab and on the job, I’ve built vehicle detection prototypes with Arduino, verified chip performance at Analog Devices, and worked hands-on with bus maintenance during OJT.

Whether it’s a circuit on a breadboard, a React storefront, or a Power BI dashboard, I like taking things from idea to working product — and making them look good along the way.
